    Weather

    Hot and humid, with showers. It gets drier as December starts.

    Langkawi · 27 Nov–1 Dec

    23–32°C

    • Showers on many November days, often short
    • Sea is about 29°C, perfect for swimming
    • About 6–7 hours of sun a day

    Penang · 1–5 Dec

    22–31°C

    • Wettest of the trip: rain on roughly half the days
    • Usually afternoon or evening downpours
    • Mornings are your best outdoor time

    Kuala Lumpur · 5–10 Dec

    24–32°C

    • Brief, heavy showers, often late afternoon
    • Very humid; malls and trains are cold
    • Batu Caves and Putrajaya: go early

        Between cities

        Flying is in the plan by default. Tap “Use this” on another option and the day plan updates for both of you.

        KLIA → Langkawi

        Fri 27 Nov

        • FlyBest

          AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines or Batik Air. Separate ticket from your long-haul flight: leave ~3 hrs between them.

          ~1 hr 10 minRM50–200

        • Train + ferry

          ETS train north, then the ~1 hr 15 min ferry from Kuala Perlis. A whole day; only for slow travellers.

          9–11 hrsRM110–150

        Langkawi → Penang

        Tue 1 Dec

        • FlyBest

          AirAsia or Firefly. The easiest way by far.

          ~40 minfrom ~RM110

        • Ferry + Grab

          Ferry to Kuala Perlis (~1 hr 15 min), Grab or bus to Butterworth (~2 hrs), then the short ferry to George Town.

          ~5 hrs~RM100–150

        • Direct ferryNot running

          Suspended for years. Old listings still show it, so don’t book it.

          ——

        Penang → Kuala Lumpur

        Sat 5 Dec

        • Fly

          Lands at KLIA, then the KLIA Ekspres (~30 min) into the city. Or Subang with Firefly.

          ~1 hr + airportRM90–260

        • ETS trainBest

          Butterworth to KL Sentral, centre to centre. Book on the KTMB app; take the ferry or a Grab to Butterworth first.

          ~4 hrsRM59–91

        • Express bus

          From Penang Sentral or Sungai Nibong to TBS. Cheapest, but slowest.

          ~5 hrs~RM40

        In each city

        Langkawi

        Barely any buses. Grab (book early) or rent a car for a day. Your villa is ~10 min from the airport and Cenang.

        Penang

        Grab is cheap. Bus 101 → ESCAPE. Bus 204 → Penang Hill & Kek Lok Si.

        KL

        Touch 'n Go card for trains (cards can't tap the gates yet). Rapid Kembara pass: RM25/day or RM55/3 days.

        KL → airport

        KLIA Ekspres from KL Sentral, ~30 min, RM55.

        KL → Malacca

        Komuter to TBS, then bus ~2.5 hrs, from RM12. Book the return.

        Before you go

        Entry

        Visa-free for Egyptian & UK passports per most sources; confirm on the official site. Passport valid 6+ months.

        Arrival card

        Fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card online within 3 days before landing.

        Book early

        Your KL hotel, the Langkawi→Penang flight on 1 Dec, the ETS train on 5 Dec, ESCAPE and SkyCab.

        Dress

        Shoulders and knees covered at mosques and temples. Mosques lend robes.
