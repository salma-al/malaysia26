          Between cities

          Flying is the default. “Use this” updates the plan for you both.

          KLIA → Langkawi

          Fri 27 Nov

          • FlyBest

            AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines or Batik Air. Separate ticket from your long-haul flight: leave ~3 hrs between them.

            ~1 hr 10 minRM50–200

          • Train + ferry

            ETS train north, then the ~1 hr 15 min ferry from Kuala Perlis. A whole day; only for slow travellers.

            9–11 hrsRM110–150

          Langkawi → Penang

          Tue 1 Dec

          • FlyBest

            AirAsia or Firefly. The easiest way by far.

            ~40 minfrom ~RM110

          • Ferry + Grab

            Ferry to Kuala Perlis (~1 hr 15 min), Grab or bus to Butterworth (~2 hrs), then the short ferry to George Town.

            ~5 hrs~RM100–150

          Penang → Kuala Lumpur

          Sat 5 Dec

          • Fly

            Lands at KLIA, then the KLIA Ekspres (~30 min) into the city. Or Subang with Firefly.

            ~1 hr + airportRM90–260

          • ETS trainBest

            Butterworth to KL Sentral, centre to centre. Book on the KTMB app; take the ferry or a Grab to Butterworth first.

            ~4 hrsRM59–91

          Weather

          Hot, humid, with showers. Drier into December.

          Langkawi · 27 Nov–1 Dec

          23–30°C

          • Short showers, sea ~29°C
          • 6–7 hrs of sun a day

          Penang · 1–5 Dec

          22–31°C

          • Wettest stop: rain most afternoons
          • Go out in the mornings

          Kuala Lumpur · 5–10 Dec

          24–32°C

          • Heavy late-afternoon showers
          • Humid outside, cold AC inside

          Entry

          Visa-free for Egyptian and UK passports (confirm officially). Passport valid 6+ months.

          Fill in the Digital Arrival Card online, within 3 days of landing.
