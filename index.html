Everything on the wishlist

Day by day

Includes the Ipoh overnight and a Malacca day trip on Sunday 6 Dec, so you catch the Jonker Street night market (Friday to Sunday only).

    If you eat halal: Malay and mamak (Indian-Muslim) places are halal. Many Chinese hawker stalls are not, but halal versions of most dishes are easy to find, especially in Penang.

    Before you book

    No ferry from Langkawi to Penang

    The direct ferry is cancelled. Take the short flight on 30 Nov, or a ferry to Kuala Perlis or Kuala Kedah plus a bus or train.

    Entry

    Most current sources say Egyptian passports are visa-free for tourism, up to 90 days. One still says a visa is needed, so confirm on the official immigration site. Passport valid 6 months past the trip, proof of the return flight, and the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card filled online in the 3 days before landing.

    Arrival-day buffer

    If the international flight and the Langkawi flight are separate tickets, allow about 3 hours at KLIA for immigration, bags and checking in again.

    Weather

    Expect afternoon showers, so plan outdoor things for mornings. Skip the east coast islands (Perhentian, Redang, Tioman): they close for the monsoon from November to February.

    Getting around

    Grab (ride-hailing app) in every city. ETS train from Butterworth (Penang) to Ipoh to KL Sentral. Bus to Malacca, about 2 hours each way.

    ESCAPE Penang tips

    Open 10:00 to 18:00, last entry 16:00. Go at opening and plan 4 to 6 hours. Register your face for check-in before the visit. Halal food and prayer rooms on site.

    Swaps if you change your mind

    Instead of the Malacca day

    Genting Highlands

    A second adventure day at Genting SkyWorlds theme park, 26 rides across 9 themed zones, cooler mountain air. About 1 hour from KL plus a cable car up. Weekdays are less crowded.

    Instead of Ipoh or a KL day

    Cameron Highlands

    Tea plantations, cool weather and strawberry farms. A long, winding 3 to 4 hour drive from KL.

    Any Langkawi afternoon

    More Langkawi adventure

    Mangrove kayaking or a jungle zipline, booked locally.

    Original notes, corrected
