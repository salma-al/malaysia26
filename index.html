    Weather

    Hot and humid, with showers. It gets drier as December starts.

    Langkawi · 26–30 Nov

    23–32°C

    • Showers on many November days, often short
    • Sea is about 29°C, perfect for swimming
    • About 6–7 hours of sun a day

    Penang & Ipoh · 30 Nov–5 Dec

    22–31°C

    • Wettest of the trip: rain on roughly half the days
    • Usually afternoon or evening downpours
    • Mornings are your best outdoor time

    Kuala Lumpur · 5–10 Dec

    24–32°C

    • Brief, heavy showers, often late afternoon
    • Very humid; malls and trains are cold
    • Batu Caves and Putrajaya: go early

    Getting around

    No Uber in Malaysia: use Grab.

    Grab · rides & foodinDrive · cheaper backup in KLKTMB · train ticketsGoogle Maps · buses & trainseSIM or KLIA SIM for data

    KLIA → Langkawi

    ~1 hr flight. Separate tickets: allow ~3 hrs. AirAsia flies from Terminal 2.

    Langkawi

    Barely any buses. Grab (book early) or rent a car for a day.

    Langkawi → Penang

    Short flight. The direct ferry is cancelled.

    Penang

    Grab is cheap. Bus 101 → ESCAPE. Bus 204 → Penang Hill & Kek Lok Si.

    Penang → Ipoh → KL

    ETS train from Butterworth (ferry or Grab there). ~2 hrs, then ~2.5 hrs. Book on KTMB.

    KL

    Touch 'n Go card for trains (cards can't tap the gates yet). Rapid Kembara pass: RM25/day or RM55/3 days.

    KL → airport

    KLIA Ekspres from KL Sentral, ~30 min, RM55.

    KL → Malacca

    Komuter to TBS, then bus ~2.5 hrs, from RM12. Book the return.

    Before you go

    Entry

    Visa-free for Egyptian & UK passports per most sources; confirm on the official site. Passport valid 6+ months.

    Arrival card

    Fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card online within 3 days before landing.

    Book early

    Langkawi→Penang flight, ETS trains, ESCAPE and SkyCab online.

    Dress

    Shoulders and knees covered at mosques and temples. Mosques lend robes.
